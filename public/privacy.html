Arabesk TV - Privacy Policy

Published by RM Apps. Last updated: November 13, 2026

What the app is

Arabesk TV is a TV player app. It plays the live channels, movies and series of a subscription that the user has with Arabesk customer service. The app works only after the user's device is activated with an activation code.

Information we collect

Device ID. To give each TV one fixed activation code, the app reads the device's ID and stores it on our server together with the activation code. It is used only to keep the app activated on that device.

Subscription login. When customer service activates a device, the subscription username and password for the TV service are stored on our server with that device's activation code, so the app can play the user's channels.

Approximate location. The app uses the device's internet (IP) address to show the city, local time and weather on the home screen and the channel info bar. We do not use GPS and we do not store a location history.

On the device only. Favorites, watch progress, language and theme settings are saved on the TV itself, not on our server.

Technical logs. Short error logs (for example, when a channel fails to play) may be kept for a few days to fix problems.

What we do not do

The app has no ads, no tracking and no analytics services. We do not sell or share personal information with anyone. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or payment details in the app.

Keeping and deleting information

Activation information is kept while the device is in use. To have a device's information deleted, contact us with the activation code shown in the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.
